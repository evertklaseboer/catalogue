# catalogue
Online version of my worldwide postage stamp 
catalogue 1840-1920, with an emphasis on 
forgeries, locals and other things that are 
difficult to find in ordinary stamp catalogue. 

****************************************
*                                      *
*            Evert Klaseboer           *
*       Postage Stamp Catalogue        *
*                                      *
****************************************

I decided to make a stamp catalogue some 
25 years ago, after I realized that 'normal'
catalogues didn't have much information
concerning forgeries, local stamps etc.
My main interest is in stamps issued
from 1840 to roughly 1920 (worldwide). 

After working on it for many years, I
now have added all information on stamps
worldwide. I've divided the catalogue
into two parts (since I initially started
with two cd's):

Volume 1: US, Great Britain, France and
Germany and all their colonies and areas.

Volume 2: The rest of the world: Benelux,
Spain, Portugal, Italy Eastern and Southern
Europe, Russia, South America etc.  

In this online catalogue, you will only
be able to see about 1 in 10 images.

Please contact me if you wish to obtain
the full 3 Gb version.
