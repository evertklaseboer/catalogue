Other overprints, such as "Kigoma" or "Karema" are bogus overprints, unofficially made by local postmasters.

In 1924 more stamps with overprint of Belgian Congo with overprint "RUANDA URUNDI" were issued. Ruanda-Urundi got it own stamps with inscription "RUANDA-URUNDI" in 1931.


1931 stamp with "PAQUEBOT" cancel.

 


Identical flower issues for Ruanda-Urundi and Belgian Congo of 1953.

Ruanda-Urundi became independent in 1962 and formed two countries, Rwanda and Burundi.
