(Forged Sinkiang overprint)


Forged overprint made by Gee Ma in the 1950's in Australia. Gee Ma (David Allen Gee) made forgeries of rare overprints and cancels from China, Tibet, Burma, Japanese occupation of several countries etc. Gee Ma also operated under false names among them: James Bond, Robert Lowe, Dr.K.Hayes, Peter Dierking and Kee On Yu.

More overprints were made after 1920 for this province.

Railway stamps

I have seen the values 0f10 green on green, 0f20 blue on blue, 0f30 brown on yellow and 0f40 red on lilac. The value 2 F violet on lilac should also exist. The value inscription is always in the colour red.
