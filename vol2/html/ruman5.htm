Forgery

The above stamps have been forged! In the forgeries there is a leaf missing left above the value inscription. For more info see: http://home.no.net/bhb1/ro-f06e.htm. These forgeries also originated from Belgium. The letters "TI" are smaller than the rest of the word "TIMBRU".
If my information is correct, a second type of forgeries exists, with the open space of the "R" of "TIMBRU" much smaller and other minor differences.
