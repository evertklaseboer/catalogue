Tanger Fez issue

1892 Palmtree


(Reduced size)

  5 c green
  10 c brown (black?)
  15 c blue
  25 c brown (black?)
  50 c red
  1 F olive
  5 F lilac


On letter together with a Spanish stamp and a Gibraltar stamp.

Forgeries exist of these stamps.


In the forgeries, the upper left side of the value-tablet does not touch the tree trunk (in genuine stamps it does). Genuine stamps are perforated 13 1/2, forgeries 11 1/2 or 12 1/2 (source Focus on Forgeries by V.E.Tyler). They exist with forged double ring cancel "FEZ MAROC 17 NOV 99" (see last images above). In my view, the upper part of the "R" is also too large in the forgeries.
