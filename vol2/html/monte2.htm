I believe that the following values exist: 1 pa brown, 2 pa green, 3 pa blue, 4 pa green, 5 pa green, 8 pa violet, 9 pa brown, 10 pa orange, 20 pa lilac, 30 pa red and 40 pa violet, 50 pa brown and 70 pa green (value and overprint in black on both stamps). 1944 Stamps of Italy of 1929, overprinted 'Deutsche Militär- verwaltung Kotor' and surcharged

  'LIT. 0.50' on 10 c brown (overprint and surcharge in red)
  'LIT. 1.-' on 25 c green (overprint and surcharge in red)
  'LIT. 1.50' on 50 c violet (overprint and surcharge in red)
  'LIT. 3.-' on 30 c brown (overprint and surcharge in red)
  'LIT. 4.-' on 20 c red
  'LIT. 10.-' on 20 c red

Only 5000 sets were issued of these stamps.

A postcard of Italy in the 1929 design as above exists (30 c brown) with similar overprint 'LIT. 1.- Deutsche Militar- Verwaltung Kotor'.

 

1944 Local issue, stamps of Jugoslavia, overprinted 'Boka Kotorska', bars on 'JUGOSLAVIA' and surcharged

  '0,10 R.M.' on 3 D light brown
  '0,15 R.M.' on 3 D light brown
  '0,25 R.M.' on 4 D blue
  '0,50 R.M.' on 4 D blue

 

Some unissued stamps exist with inscription 'BOKA KOTORSKA'. I have seen the values 15 p brown, 50 p blue and 80 p red. The designs are all slightly different; sheep herdess for the 15 p, soldier for the 50 p and wine farmer woman for the 80 p.
