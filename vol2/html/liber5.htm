1909 Various designs

  1 c green and black (coffee plantation)
  2 c red and black (president Barclay)
  5 c blue and black (ship)
  10 c lilac and black (triangular stamp)
  15 c blue and black
  20 c red and green
  25 c brown and black
  30 c brown (president Barclay)
  50 c green and black (ship)
  75 c brown and black

All stamps are perforated 14. The 10 c also exists rouletted.
