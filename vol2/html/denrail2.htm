Aabenraa - Amts Jernbaner


1920 Coat of arms with 3 fishes. 90 o green. I've seen this stamp overprinted "50 Ore". A 45 o yellow also exist. Also "25 Øre" on 45 o overprint.

A 30 o blue must have been issued in 1930. I have not seen this stamp.

"GODSFRIMAERKE AALBORG PRIVATBANER" with coat of arms of Aalborg, no outer framelines


"GODSFRIMAERKE AALBORG PRIVATBANER" with coat of arms of Aalborg, with outer framelines. I've also seen 25 o green.


1919: "85 Øre." on 45 o yellow and "100 Øre" on 25 o green


1931 Very similar design but smaller size and lettering different; I've also seen 10 o blue, 25 o green, 50 o grey and 60 o red.

1941-65 Various railway related designs


Railway related pictorial designs: I've also seen a 50 o with an image of a different train. Also a 50 c with the image of a 'trainbus' and another 50 c with the image of a train. Also 330 o with image of a bridge and "225 ORE" on 330 o.

Several more pictorial designs were issued, such as 60 o image of a house, 125 o (coat of arms of Aalborg), 75 o (map of the railway, even in two different types, one with a little ship added), 200 o (statue of a bull). Also image of a bridge: 2 Kr and "225 ØRE" on 2 Kr. .

See also: http://www.vodskov-posthistorie.dk/Plancher/Oversigt/OVS-Jernbanemaerker.htm

Aalborg - Hadsund -Jernbane


1900: Coat of arms of Aalborg, wild boar and tree. 10 o green. I've also seen 20 o red.

Aarhus - Hammel - Thorso


25 o red. I've also seen a 25 o brown in this design with a 1917 cancel. The Hammel -Aarhus Railway issued very similar stamps.

In this type I have seen:
With large "ØRE": 25 o red, 60 o light blue
With small "Øre" 25 o red, 50 o orange, 60 o light blue, 80 o green.
