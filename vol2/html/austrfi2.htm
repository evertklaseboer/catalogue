1850 "Ankundigungs- Stempel.' (tax on announcements)


"Ankundigungs-Stempel."; tax on announcements. The values 1/2 k, 1 k and 2 k exist. Also with "1870" added in the design.

 

1850 Arms with "C.M." at the bottom


1850 issue, with "C.M."

This or similar designs, with inscription "C.M." below the value was issued in 1850. The following values exist: 1 k, 2 k, 3 k, 6 k , 10 k, 15 k, 30 k, 45 k, 1 Fl, 2 Fl, 3 Fl, 4 Fl, 5 Fl, 6 Fl, 8 Fl, 10 Fl, 12 Fl, 14 Fl, 16 Fl, 18 Fl and 20 Fl. The larger values are larger. All values are in green and black, except the 1 k which is green and orange and the 2 k which is printed in green and red.
Similar stamps, but with inscription 'centesimi' or 'Lire' were issued in Austrian Italy. Also stamps in red and black were issued for Austrian Italy.

 

1858 As 1850 issue, but without "C.M."

In 1858 this set was re-issued, without the inscription "C.M".


1858 "C.M." removed

In this design the following values exist (all in black with grey background and slightly different designs) 1/2 k, 1 k, 2 k, 3 k, 4 k, 5 k, 6 k, 7 k, 10 k, 12 k, 15 k, 25 k, 30 k, 36 k, 50 k, 60 k, 72 k, 75 k, 90 k, 1 Fl, 2 Fl, 2.50 Fl, 3 Fl, 4 Fl, 5 Fl, 6 Fl, 7 Fl, 8 Fl, 10 Fl, 12 Fl, 14 Fl, 15 Fl, 16 Fl, 18 Fl and 20 Fl.


Austrian Italy issue with inscription in "Centesimi". Also a black and red background for Austrian Italy.

 

1863 With value written in words at the bottom of the stamp
