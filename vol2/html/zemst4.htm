Forgery (left) based on an illustration of the Moens catalogue (right) "Les Timbres-Poste Ruraux de Russie" by Samuel Koprowski (editor J.B.Moens), 1875. In the word 'RYZANSKO' (upper left), the letters 'R' and '3' are inverted. Source: http://ufdc.ufl.edu/UF00020235/00035/23j. It also appears already in the John Edward Gray 'The Illustrated Catalogue of Postage Stamps' catalogue of 1870 on page 181 (see image above). Note tht the bottom and top part are both missing in the above illustrations.
