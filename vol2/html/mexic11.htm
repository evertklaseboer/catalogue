Stamps with overprint 'E.G de M' are of private origin. Other 'dubious' overprints exist, such as: 'GBNO CONST', 'Gob. Cons.', also 'GOBIERNO CONSTITUCIONALISTA' different from the ones listed below (some with extra letters added).

All the above stamps exist with 'OFICIAL' overprint. Also with 'OFICIAL' and 'G.P.DE M' overprint at the same time.


('OFICIAL' overprint on 5 P)

These stamps exist overprinted with large 'GCM' in fancy letters (=Gobierno Constitucionalista Mexicana' in red, violet, green or black. Three types of this overprint seem to exist:
