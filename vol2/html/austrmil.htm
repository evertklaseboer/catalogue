I suspect the next two stamps to be Fournier forgeries:

The above two stamps have the same cancel "K.U.K. ETTAPENPOSTAMT CETINJE -1 III 17" as can be found in 'The Fournier Album of Philatelic Forgeries'. I presume Fournier forged the overprint and the cancel only, not the basic stamp.


The forged 'Etappenpost' cancel and the forged overprints for Montenegro as can be found in 'The Fournier Album of Philatelic Forgeries' (reduced sizes).
