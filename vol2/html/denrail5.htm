Horve Vaerslev


Large sized stamp, 25 o blue


Smaller sized stamps: 75 o blue and black. Very similar stamps were issued for the Odsherreds Jernbane (next image).

In this smaller design I have seen: 40 o red, 75 o blue and black, "70" on 60(?) o green and black, "1 kr." on 40 o red.

De Jydsk - Fyenske Jernbaner


1870 issue 8 Sk green and 12 Sk red. Trains in each corner. Apperently a control stamp was needed before they could be used (I have never seen this control stamp).


Reprints, the design is less clear than in the originals. I've seen these reprints without the fancy monogram overprint

 


1867 Design as the Danske Statsbaner, inscrption "De jydsk-fyenske Jernbaner FOR PAKKER INDTIL 10 PUNDS VAEGT" (parcels under 10 pounds); 8 sk red. A very similar stamp exist but with inscription 8 PUNDS (also 8 sk red).

Kjøbenhavn - Slangerup Banen


25 o red (5 Kg), next to with the 'kilo' indications erased with a pen. Also 50 o blue (5 Kg).


"GODSFRIMAERKE": 25 o red, 30 o black and 50 o blue.


Inscription "SUPPLEMENTS GODSFRIMAERKE"; 10 o black on blue and 15 o red.


Inscription "GODSFRIMAERKE", smaller size 40 o green.

Koge - Ringsted Jernbane


25 o blue, the only stamp I've seen from this company. I've seen it with a 1926 cancel.

Many overprints exist for this stamp:
  "35 ØRE" (violet) on 25 o blue
  "35 ØRE" (red) on 25 o blue
  "40 *ØRE*" in a triangle on 25 o blue
  "50 ØRE." (green) on 25 o blue
  "60 ØRE" in a black box on 25 o blue
  "75 ØRE." (red) on 25 o blue
  "90 ØRE" (red) in a red ellipse on 25 o blue (see image above)
  "90" on 25 o blue

Kolding - Egtved - Jernbane


10 o red. I've seen this stamp with a 1916 cancel. I've also seen the value 45 o red.


Cancelled "GRAVENS" in 1921. I've also seen 25 o green in this design. Also a "25" on 20 o green (see image above).
