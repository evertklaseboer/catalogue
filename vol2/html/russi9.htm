1900 Stamps of Russia, overprinted with value in "PARA" or "PIASTRE"

  '4 PARA' (blue or black) on 1 k orange
  '10 PARA' (red) on 2 k green
  '20 PARA' (blue) on 4 k red
  '20 PARA' on 5 k lilac (1910)
  '1 PIASTRE' (red) on 10 k blue
  '2 PIASTRES' on 20 k blue and red
  '5 PIASTRES' (red) on 50 k lilac and green
  '7 PIASTRES' (blue) on 70 k brown and orange
  '10 PIASTRES' (blue) on 1 R brown and orange (larger size)
  '35 PIASTRES' (red) on 3 1/2 R black and grey (larger size)
  '70 PIASTRES' (red) on 7 R black and yellow (larger size)

Value of the stamps

 
vc = very common
c  = common
*  = not so common
** = uncommon
 
*** = very uncommon
