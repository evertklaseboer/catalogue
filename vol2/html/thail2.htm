Some forgeries exist of this revenue stamp in a different colour (violet), see http://homepage.tinet.ie/~saparker/tps/ttAug2003_Forgeries.htm. They were probably made with a computer scanner and printer and offered on Ebay (2004) by a seller from Hialeah Florida (they are referred to as Hialeah-forgeries). This forger also made other forgeries of Thailand of more modern stamps (see the above mentioned website for more details).
