The next forgeries are all offered on old (genuine?) documents on Ebay (2005). The cancels seem quite dubious to me. I presume they were all made by the same forger, but I don't know who made these forgeries.

Forgeries of Tuscany:


This is a badly executed forgery of the 1 cr value of the 1851 issue of Tuscany, it has a pencancel, I've seen several of these forgeries.


An old document with such a forgery

I've only seen the 1 cr forgery of Tuscany.

I've also seen a yellow newspaper stamp with the 'DISTRIBUZIONE III' cancel as in the newspaper tax stamps shown below.
