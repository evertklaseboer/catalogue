1916 Stamps of Rio de Oro (1915 issue) overprinted "CABO JUBI" and value

  '5 CENTIMOS' (blue) on 4 p red
  '10 CENTIMOS' (red) on 10 p violet
  '15 CENTIMOS' (red or green) on 50 c brown
  '40 CENTIMOS' (red or green) on 1 P lilac

Value of the stamps

 
vc = very common
c  = common
*  = not so common
** = uncommon
 
*** = very uncommon
R   = rare
RR  = very rare
RRR = extremely rare
Value Unused Used Remarks
5 c on 4 p RR RR  
10 c on 10 p RR RR  
15 c on 50 p RR RR  
40 c on 1 P RR RR  

The stamps 5 c on 4 p and 10 c on 10 p exist with double overprint (RR), the 10 c on 10 p even exists with a green and red overprint (RRR).
