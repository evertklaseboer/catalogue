Varde N.Nebel


1903 Lion with crown in a circle, inscription "VARDE V.N.J. N.NEBEL GODSFRIMAERKE"


1913 Lion with crown in a circle, inscription "VARDE N.NEBEL TARM. GODSFRIMAERKE"

 

There are several types of the above stamps:
"V.N.J" on top
Broad letters at the bottom, especially the "G", "S" and "R" of "GODSFRIMAERKE": 10 o green, 20 o red (solid background behind lion), "10" on 20 o red,
Narrow letter at the bottom, especially the "G", "S" and "R" of "GODSFIRMAERKE", no dot behind this word: 25 o orange
"N.NEBEL" on top:
Without dot behind "GODSFRIMAERKE" and tall "N.NEBEL": 10 green, 25 o orange, "45" (violet) on 25 o orange
With dot behind "GODSFRIMAERKE" and less tall "N.NEBEL": 35 o green, 60 o red, 90 o violet, "75" (violet) on 60 o red, "75" on 90 o violet

I've seen a commemorative cover (Jubilaeumskuvert) with an image of a 20 o red and a "10" on 20 red printed side by side. It has inscription "VESTBANEN 15.marts 1903 1978".


In this design I have seen 25 o brown, 40 o blue, 60 o red, 75 o green, 90 o yellow.
