1919 Fiscal stamps of Austria 'FINANZVERWALTUNG ALBANIENS' overprinted 'POSTA Shkodres SHQYPNIS' or 'POSTAT SHQIPTARE'

With 'XVIMCMX' overprinted in red or blue

  (2) q on 2 h brown
  05 q on 16 h green
  10 q on 8 h red
  25 q on 64 h blue (2 types, eagle different)
  50 q on 32 h violet
  1 F on 1.28 Kr yellow on blue

All stamps have a small eagle, but the 64 h also exists with a larger eagle (with no shadow on its body).
