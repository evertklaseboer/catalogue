Postal forgery:

In the above postal forgery, the 'S' and 'T' of 'IMPUESTO' are not joined (they should be in the genuine stamps. The ear is too large and the inscription below the neck reads 'NLIA' instead of 'JULIA'. This forgery has been described in 'The Postal Forgeries of the World' by H.G. Leslie Fletcher. If my information is correct, this forgery was used in Barcelona.

BARCELONA

An extra tax was obligatory from 1929 to 1945 in Barcelona.

1929 Inscription 'EXPOSICION INTERNACIONAL BARCELONA 1930'

Inscription '1929':
  5 c blue ('Serie 1a')
  5 c red and lilac ('Serie 2a')
  5 c brown and yellow ('Serie 3a')

Inscription '1930'
  5 c green (two shades, 'Serie 4a')
  5 c violet and lilac ('Serie 5a')
  5 c black and yellow ('Serie 6a')
Similar stamps with inscription '1930' and 'C.F.y.E.F' 
instead of 'Serie x' were issued in 1930 for a philatelic exhibtion (
'Congreso Filatelico y Exposicion'): 
  5 c violet and blue 
  5 c blue and lilac
