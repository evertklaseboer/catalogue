20 r stamp with red "IMPOSTO" overprint; fiscal stamp (reduced size)

I have seen the '18 AVOS' on 20 r lilac (REPUBLICA red) stamp with additional overprint '1 avo' and "IMPOSTO DO SELLO" with bars on the '18 AVOS' value. I've seen a similar overprint on the '18 AVOS' on 75 r red (REPUBLICA red). Other values exist. These fiscal stamps were issued in 1920(?).

I've also seen a smaller "IMPOSTO DO SELLO" overprint on the '2' and bars on '6 AVOS' on 80 r green stamp. Other values exist. These fiscal stamps were issued in 1920(?).
