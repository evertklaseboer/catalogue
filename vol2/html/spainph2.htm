Local manual overprint for Zamboanga "C. 1898 RESELLADO 1899 T":


I've also seen 1 m,. 5 c, 10 c and 1 P. I've never seen any used stamps.

Some stamp stocks had fallen in the hands of the insurgents and therefore the above overprinted stamps were prepared to distinguish them from other stamps. However, these overprinted stamps were never set in use.

Later Issues

In 1899 the Philippines were occupied by the United States (listed in the Cd with Volume 1). During World War II, Japan occupied the Philippines and issued special stamps.
