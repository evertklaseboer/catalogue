1915 Various designs, eagle, famous persons, map and buildings

  1 c violet (arms, eagle, 2 types)
  2 c green (statue of Cuauhtemoc)
  3 c brown (Zaragoza)
  4 c red (Morelos)
  5 c orange (Madero)
  10 c blue (Juarez)
  40 c grey (map of Mexico)
  40 c violet (1917)
  1 P brown and black (Veracruz lighthouse)
  1 P blue and black (1917)
  5 P red and blue (Head post office in Mexico city)
  5 P green and grey (1917)

The values 1 c, 2 c, 3 c, 4 c, 5 c, and 10 c exist rouletted. All values exist perforated.
