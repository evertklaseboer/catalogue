Fournier made forgeries of these stamps. He offers 9 values (from 10 to 1000 reis) as first choice forgeres for 4 Swiss francs in his 1914 Pricelist. He also offers 8 values (the other than red coloured stamps?) for 3 Swiss Francs in the same pricelist. He made forgeries of both the 1899 red coloured stamps and the 1900 other coloured stamps. These forgeries are presented in the Fournier Album in vertical rows, the lower value on top and the higher values printed below it. I've seen whole sheets of his forgeries constisting of rows of 6 stamps of each value, with all values printed on the same sheet. There is an empty row on the coloured 1900 issue, where the 100 r value should be. Fournier used the following cancels on these forgeries ('O DO RIO GRANDE DO SUL 29 OUT 1908 3AS.' and 'ADM. DO SC DO PARANA (EXP.) 18 JUL 92'):

War Labels


(Reduced size)

Labels with inscription 'EXERCITO EM OPERACOES CONTRA O PARAGUAY' were issued by Brazil during the war against Paraguay in 1865. None of these labels were actually used for postal purposes. I have seen the colours: black on red, black on lilac, black on yellow, black on violet and black on blue. These labels are quite rare (RR).

Non issued stamps, ACRE

1899 Inscription 'ESTADO INDEPENDENTE DO ACRE PATRIA E LIBERDADE 1899':


(Reduced size)

According to 'Les timbres de phantasie' of Georges Chapier (in French), these stamps were prepared for a country named Acre, nowadays in Brazil near Bolivia. This republic became independant for a very short time, but was never able to use these already prepared stamps. The following values seem to exist: 200 r, 300 r, 500 r and 800 r in the design with a tree, house and star and 2000 r and 5000 r in the design of a flag.
