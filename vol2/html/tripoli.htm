Stamps of Italy with overprint 'TRIPOLITANIA' were issued from 1923 onwards. The first stamps with inscription 'TRIPOLITANIA' or 'TRIPOLI' were issued in 1926.

1923 300th anniversary of the propagation of faith ('III CENTANARIO DI PROGAGANDA FIDE' stamps of Italy, overprinted 'TRIPOLITANIA'

  20 c green and brown
  30 c red and brown
  50 c violet and brown
  1 L blue and brown
