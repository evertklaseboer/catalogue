Forgeries:


Genuine and forgery

Forgeries exist, They can be recognized by the following characteristics:

1) There is a large defect in the scroll work above the last "R" of "RECEBER"
2) The lettering is much more crude on the forgery.
3) There are color differences in the forgery.

Information and images obtained with permission from the Forgeries idenfication site of Bill Claghorn http://geocities.com/claghorn1p/. I have also seen the values 30 r, 130 r, 200 r and 500 r of this particular forgery (also cancelled). Probably the whole serie exist.
