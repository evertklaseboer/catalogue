These stamps were overprinted "medio real", "un real" and "dos reales" to be used in Comayagua and Tegucigalpa with or without additional frame (*** to RRR).


(Genuine overprint)

 

The blue stamp (see above) is a forgery, the colour is wrong, it misses the two stars and the branches in the design (it is the third forgery described in Album Weeds, I have seen this same forgery in the colours red and green). It has the cancel "CORREOS 7.1.6? II-III'", which also exists on forgeries of other countries (Uruguay for example). This is the third forgery described in Album Weeds. It also exists with a "FRANCO" in a box cancel. There are at least 6 forgeries of these stamps, I don't know if the other stamp is genuine.


Two other forgeries, the first one is very primitive, the second one (in red color) is made by the same forger who produced the blue forgery shown above.


"CORREOS" forgeries.

Other forgeries:


Forgery with second "N" of "UNION" almost touching the pyramid. I've also seen this forgery in black on red. The arch connecting the two towers does not have a central line. The left star is badly done. This is most likely the first forgery described in Album Weeds.


Forgeries, reduced sizes


Other forgery


Forgery with very large left star and broad "Y". The catalogue of Placido Ramon de Torres "Album Illustrado para Sellos de Correo" of 1879 also has a very similar image on page 165 (information passed to me thanks to Gerhard Lang, 2016).


More forgeries.
