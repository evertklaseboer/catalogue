Many Portuguese colonies issued a newspaper stamp around 1894 in the value 2 1/2 r brown. The following colonies issued this stamp (and many of them with overprints): Angola, Cape Verde, Lorenzo Marques, Macao, Mozambique, Mozambique Company (overprinted on Mozambique), Portuguese Congo, Portuguese Guinea, St Thomas and Prince, Timor and Zambezia
