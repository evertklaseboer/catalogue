Cancels:
Before 1869 Angra used the numeral cancel "48". Horta used "49", Ponta Delgada used "50" (all with more than 2 interrupted lines)
After 1869 Angra used the numeral cancel "42". Horta used "43", Ponta Delgada used "44" (all with 2 interrupted lines).
