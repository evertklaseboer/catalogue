The civil war between the nationalists and replubicans lasted from 1936 to 1939 in Spain. Many provisional stamps were issued. However, there is a certain number of stamps that was issued for stamp collectors only. I will give some examples here, without any intention of being complete:

'Asociacion de Caridad Sello de Franqueo Obligatorio Viva Espana', I've also seen a 5 c (same colours and design)

'HUEVAR VIVA ESPANA 18-7-37' etc on normal stamps of Spain"

'ISLAS CANARIAS' and 'CONTRA PARO OBRERO LOGRONO'

'Junta patriotica do norte assistencia'

'Correos Espana Pro beneficencia huevar'

 


In this design ('CORREOS ESPANA PRO BENEFICENCIA HUEVAR'), I have seen the values 5 c brown, 5 c green, 10 c red and 10 c violet.

'Franqueo obligatorio Loja'


'ASOCIACION DE CARIDAD JOJA' 5 c red with 'Franqueo' overprint, I've also seen the 5 c red without overprint, a 5 c brown, and this 5 c brown with a 'Aniversario liberacion 18-8-37' overprint

'Assistencia social correos Espana antequera'


I've seen the values 5 c brown on yellow (perforated or rouletted) and 5 c blue on yellow (rouletted)
