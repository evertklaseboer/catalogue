Allied occupation

1943 Stamp issued during the Allied Military Occupation (end of World War II), inscription 'ALLIED MILITARY POSTAGE ITALY'

In this design exist: 
  15 c orange (date of issue 24 August 1943)
  25 c olive (date of issue 17 September 1943)
  30 c brown (date of issue 17 September 1943)
  50 c lilac (date of issue 17 September 1943)
  60 c yellow (date of issue 15 October 1943)
  1 L green (date of issue 17 September 1943)
  2 L red (date of issue 14 October 1943)
  5 L blue (date of issue 20 October 1943)
  10 L brown (date of issue 20 October 1943)

All stamps have 'ITALY' and 'CENTESIMI', 'LIRA' or 'LIRE' in black. These stamps were declared invalid on 30 September 1944.
